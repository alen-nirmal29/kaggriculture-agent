# V1 strategy

V1 manages a deterministic six-tile route in the unlocked NW quadrant: `(4,4)`, `(3,4)`, `(2,4)`, `(1,4)`, `(1,3)`, `(2,3)`.

It grows carrots because the installed rules give an estimated $85 profit over a three-day max-yield cycle, a stronger simple labor-adjusted return than wheat's estimated $90 over four days. Six plots leave enough of each 24-turn day to traverse the route and service every crop, including the extra harvest/replant/water actions on turnover days.

The task hierarchy harvests mature crops, waters every live crop daily, clears weeds, plants empty managed plots, deposits carried goods when practical, and sells all shed produce. New investments stop when there is insufficient season time for payback; the final day focuses on harvest, deposit, and sale.
